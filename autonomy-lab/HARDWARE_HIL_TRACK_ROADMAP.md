# Hardware/HIL/Closed-track research roadmap

The independent lab raises software research maturity without pretending to raise physical qualification scores. The roadmap is:

1. independent source/scenario/oracle baseline;
2. deterministic parameter exploration;
3. ASAM bridge manifest and external simulator schema validation;
4. CARLA/esmini execution adapter with reproducible scenario hashes;
5. virtual-to-HIL traceability and bench stimulus definitions;
6. target-computer soak and field diagnostics on real hardware;
7. physical HIL execution with synchronized measurements;
8. Vietnam controlled-track pack at an approved facility;
9. independent safety/cybersecurity/hardware/HMI review;
10. production-readiness decision based on evidence, not simulation score.

No step authorizes public-road operation or actuator control.

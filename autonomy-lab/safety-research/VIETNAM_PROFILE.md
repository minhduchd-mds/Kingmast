# Vietnam safety research profile

Vietnam-focused virtual research is grounded in Law 36/2024/QH15 and the applicable technical-safety regulation references in the source registry. The profile does not infer that U.S. or EU thresholds are valid for Vietnam; it uses common safety concepts only to generate research questions.

Priority scenario themes:

- dense motorcycle interactions and blind-zone occupancy;
- weak/faded lane markings and temporary roadworks geometry;
- rain/spray and high-contrast exposure transitions;
- GNSS degradation/recovery in synthetic urban-canyon conditions;
- vulnerable-road-user occlusion by large vehicles;
- warning arbitration under simultaneous hazards;
- speed/sign/map disagreement;
- read-only vehicle authority and human driver responsibility.

Physical execution remains limited to an approved closed facility after HIL/target prerequisites and independent review.

# Independent safety research source tiers

Priority order for new source candidates:

1. law/regulation and regulator publications;
2. standards-body public abstracts/specification pages;
3. independent consumer-safety assessment protocols;
4. OEM owner manuals and official technical/product material;
5. other public research only as supporting context.

OEM material never outranks regulation or independent standards in the research registry. A feature existing on an OEM vehicle is not evidence that KINGMAST implements or validates that feature equivalently.

# Research score policy

`recognitionResearchScore` is intentionally a simulation-coverage metric. It may be used to compare revisions of the independent research library, but it must not be displayed or described as a crash probability, reliability percentage, regulatory score, NCAP score, ASIL level, homologation status or real-world safety percentage.

Any UI or report that exposes this score must pair it with the research-only qualification claim and physical-evidence limitations.
